A few words about me...

Education

I studied Physics, followed by a Master's and PhD specialising in experimental nuclear physics. During my PhD, I worked as a doctoral student at CERN, where I remained after as a postgraduate working in radiation protection.

Information design

In research I realised that the hardest part of a problem is not solving it but actually explaining it. How do you take something complex and make it land? This question brought me to information design.

Hobbies

Outside of work, I enjoy creative projects like illustration, branding and 3D modelling. I also enjoy board games, video games, books, and listening to or playing music.

Fun fact

During a night shift during my PhD, I wrote a script that displayed jokes on a screen in an infinite loop for my colleagues to discover in the morning. It's apparently still running.

What I bring...

Analytical thinking

My research background taught me to break complex problems into smaller, understandable pieces before looking for solutions.

Curiosity

I thrive exploring new ideas, tools and disciplines. Learning is one of my favourite parts of any project and I feel accomplished when I can truly understand something.

Empathy

Behind every visualisation, interface or presentation is a person trying to understand something. I keep that person in mind and make learning approachable and enjoyable.

Making things

I enjoy taking ideas from concept to reality and seeing what the final outcome actually looks and feels like.
