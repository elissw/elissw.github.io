Creating chemical elements: One neutron at a time

Design goals

  • Explain the concept of stellar nucleosynthesis and especially how the s-process works.
  • Present the astrophysical information and mechanisms without overwhelming the reader with excessive detail.
  • Link the astrophysical information to the experimental nuclear physics work that happens in a lab.
  • Use the infographic format to make a complex topic easier to follow.
  • Employ a visually engaging style by combining vintage illustrations and typography with modern elements.

Data & Methodology

  • Written from my doctoral research in nuclear astrophysics. Simplified while remaining accurate.
  • Infographic created using Inkscape. Vintage illustrations are from public domain sources.
